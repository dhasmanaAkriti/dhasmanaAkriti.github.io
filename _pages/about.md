Hi! I am Akriti, a first-year PhD student in the Computer Science Department at the University of Colorado Boulder, where I am advised by [Dr. Maria Antoniak](https://maria-antoniak.github.io/) and [Dr. Alexis Palmer](https://www.colorado.edu/linguistics/alexis-palmer). I  conduct research as part of the [Culture, Language and Systems Lab](https://maria-antoniak.github.io/antoniak-lab) and the [Low-Resource and Endangered Languages and Computational Semantics group](https://lecs-lab.github.io/). Before joining CU Boulder, I completed my Master's in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Notre Dame, where I was advised by [Dr. David Chiang](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~dchiang/). As a part of the NLP Lab at Notre Dame, I worked on Automated Speech Recognition for low-resource Indic dialects and language varieties with [Dr Chiang](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~dchiang/) and [Aarohi Srivastava](https://aarsri.github.io/).


My current research interests lie in cultural analytics, AI-assisted fiction writing, low-resource NLP and automated speech recognition. Broadly, my research interests are as follows:
- **Cultural Analytics & Narrative Understanding**: Advancing computational tools for improving narrative understanding and cultural analytics for texts written in low-resource languages.
- **Low Resource NLP**: Improving the scope, accessibility, and quality of various NLP and ASR tools for low-resource linguistic communities.
- **AI Assisted Fiction Writing**: Developing tools for writers who work in multilingual and multimodal creative spaces.
